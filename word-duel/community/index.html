Community Guidelines

Effective: September 16, 2026

Play hard. Keep it respectful.

These rules apply to online duels, friend-room names, secret words, and interactions connected to Word Duel.

Do

  • Choose real dictionary words and room names suitable for a general audience age 13 and older.
  • Play fairly and report serious cheating, harassment, or offensive names.
  • Use the block control when you do not want to be paired with a player again.

Do not

  • Use slurs, threats, sexual content, harassment, hate speech, or personally identifying information.
  • Cheat, automate guesses, exploit defects, interfere with another match, or evade a block or enforcement action.
  • Submit false or repeated reports to target another player.

Reports, blocks, and enforcement

After an online duel, use Report to send the match and opponent identifiers to the moderation queue. Use Block to prevent future pairing. Reports are rate-limited. We may reject content, invalidate results, restrict online access, or suspend an account based on severity and history.

Appeals and urgent concerns

For an appeal or an urgent safety concern, email jefftse@gmail.com. Include your platform and the approximate date of the match, but do not send passwords or authentication credentials.
