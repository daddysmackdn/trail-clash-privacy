Word Duel: Secret Rivals

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

At a glance

Word Duel: Secret Rivals is published by Computer Office Solutions. The game is intended for players age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. This policy explains how information is handled when you play on Android or iOS.

Information handled by the game

  • Platform identity. When you choose online play, Google Play Games or Apple Game Center may provide your verified display name and authentication credential. Unity Authentication uses that credential to create a pseudonymous player ID. Android and iOS players remain in separate platform pools.
  • Gameplay and room information. Match choices, secret words, guesses, scores, room codes, multiplayer status, blocks, and reports may be processed to operate matches, rooms, and anti-abuse features. Android and iOS players use separate matchmaking pools.
  • Local game data. Single Player progress, privacy choices, accessibility preferences, and similar gameplay settings may be stored on your device.
  • Advertising and consent data. Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostics, consent choices, age-band choice, and fraud-prevention signals. Word Duel disables the advertising SDK's publisher first-party identifier.
  • Optional analytics. If you opt in, Unity Analytics receives privacy-limited action, outcome, mode, and level categories plus standard device and session data. Secret words, guesses, display names, room codes, passwords, and player IDs are not included in custom analytics events.
  • Diagnostics. Unity Diagnostics receives an installation-specific ID, crash logs, app diagnostics, device information, and app performance data so we can find and repair stability problems. It does not receive secret words, guesses, display names, room codes, or passwords from our code.
  • Support communications. If you contact us, we receive the information in your message and your email address so we can respond.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate players, provide matches and rooms, calculate game results, remember game state, show and measure ads, honor privacy choices, prevent fraud or abuse, troubleshoot technical problems, and respond to support requests.

Advertising and privacy choices

Players who have not confirmed they are at least 13 receive ad-free Single Player. Players who confirm they are at least 13 but do not confirm they are at least 18 receive under-age advertising treatment. Players who confirm they are at least 18 receive adult treatment, and advertising may be personalized only where applicable law, platform permission, and consent permit it. You can review or change available advertising privacy choices from the game's Settings screen. You can also limit or reset advertising identifiers through Android or iOS settings. Computer Office Solutions does not directly sell personal information. Advertising partners may process or share identifiers and activity for advertising purposes as described in their own policies and subject to your available choices.

Third-party services

The game may rely on the following providers, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy, including Google Play Games, AdMob, and User Messaging Platform.
  • Unity Privacy Policy, including Unity Authentication and other enabled Unity Gaming Services.
  • Apple Privacy Policy, including Game Center and App Store distribution.

Data retention and deletion

Active and completed duel records are automatically removed 30 minutes after the last player activity. Local settings remain until they are cleared or the app is removed. Safety reports are automatically scheduled for deletion after 24 months, except where longer retention is reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, a legal obligation, or an active investigation. Reports are restricted to authorized moderation access.

Unity Analytics raw events are retained for up to 13 months. A player deletion request is normally completed by Unity within 30 days. Unity Diagnostics data is retained for up to 90 days. You can copy both your Word Duel account ID and Diagnostics installation ID from the in-game account controls for an external privacy request.

You can delete your Word Duel online account and associated game data in the app from Settings > Age, Policies & Account > Delete Account. You can also follow the account deletion instructions. Deleting Word Duel data does not delete your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account.

Children's privacy

Word Duel: Secret Rivals is intended for players age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Security and international processing

We use platform services and reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their published safeguards and applicable law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or legal requirements change. The current version will remain posted at this address with an updated date.
