Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Age, Policies & Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then tap it again within eight seconds to confirm.

What is deleted

The in-app process removes the Word Duel Unity Authentication account, private player profile, block list, active or retained duel records associated with that player, and requests deletion of Unity Analytics data when applicable. Local Word Duel policy and preference data is cleared after the online deletion succeeds. Diagnostics deletion is handled separately by installation ID.

What is not deleted

Your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account is managed by Google or Apple and is not deleted. Safety reports may be retained for up to 24 months, or longer when required for an active investigation, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Access to retained reports is restricted.

Request deletion without the app

Email jefftse@gmail.com with the subject Word Duel account deletion. In the app, open Settings, then Age, Policies & Account, and use Copy Support IDs. Include the copied Word Duel account ID and Diagnostics installation ID, whether you use Android or iOS, and your platform display name. The Diagnostics installation ID lets us request deletion of crash and performance records. Do not send your password or authentication credentials. We may ask for limited verification before deleting a record.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days unless applicable law allows or requires a different period.
